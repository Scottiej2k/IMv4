The method

You learn a language by understanding a lot of it.

That's the idea behind comprehensible input: take in large amounts of real language that you can mostly follow, pitched just above your level, and your brain does the rest. Each episode uses the Italian you already know plus a few dozen new words, so you follow the story while it stretches you a little. You spend your time reading and listening to Italian, not tapping through exercises.

The show

Episodes, not exercises.

Via dei Tigli is a street in Borgoverde, a small town north of Milan. The Carters have just moved there from Ohio, next door to a neighbour who sees everything and down the street from a grandfather who won't admit he's lonely. Like any good sitcom, it runs in seasons: 8 seasons of 25 episodes, with running jokes, secrets and a cast you get to know. You come back for the characters, and the Italian comes with them.

In every episode
