The method

Stop drilling phrases. Start understanding Italian.

Fluency comes from understanding lots of real Italian, pitched just above your level. It's called comprehensible input, and it's how you learned your first language. Every episode gives you half an hour of story you can follow, with a few new words at a time.

A reason to come back besides a streak

Words per hour are estimates. A phrase-app lesson is 12 to 15 short exercises of a few words each, repeats included, over 2 to 4 minutes. Our beginner audio runs at about 5,600 Italian words an hour, learners typically read 80 to 120 words a minute, and later episodes are spoken at natural speed.

The show

Episodes, not exercises.

Via dei Tigli is a street in Borgoverde, a small town north of Milan. The Carters have just moved there from Ohio, next door to a neighbor who sees everything and down the street from a grandfather who won't admit he's lonely. Like any good sitcom, it runs in seasons: 8 seasons of 25 episodes, with running jokes, secrets and a cast you get to know. You come back for the characters, and the Italian comes with them.

In every episode

Everything you need, around one story.

  1. Vocabulary

    The episode's new words, each with its sentence from the story. Download the list as an Anki deck for every episode.

  2. Grammar lesson

    One point per episode, explained in plain English with examples from the story.

  3. Italian story

    The episode itself, with full-cast audio that underlines each word as it's spoken. Play from any sentence, at your own speed.

  4. Side by side text

    Italian and English, sentence by sentence, whenever you want to check.

  5. Progress tracking

    Your reading and listening time for every episode, and how many words you've met.

The path

The Italian grows with you. Early episodes use short sentences in the present tense; later ones sound like Italians talking.
