This policy explains what personal information Input Masters · Italian (“Input Masters”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use our website and course, why we collect it, and the choices you have. The service is run by [business or legal name], [postal address].

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture from Google. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Clerk.
  • Subscription details. Your plan (monthly or yearly), its status and renewal date, and a customer reference from Stripe. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Learning progress. Which episodes you open, how far you read and listen, reading and listening time, the words you mark as known, and your settings (such as playback speed). This is what lets you pick up where you left off on any device.
  • Messages you send us. Your name, email address and message when you use the contact form.
  • Technical data. Basic information your browser sends, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, kept in server logs for security and to fix problems.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To take payment, manage your subscription and give you access to the episodes you're entitled to.
  • To save and sync your progress.
  • To answer your messages and send you service emails (for example about your account or subscription).
  • To keep the service working, secure and free of abuse, and to understand in aggregate how it is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show advertising.
