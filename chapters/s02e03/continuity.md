### s02e03 · Siamo andati a Monza
- **Happened:** The Carters take a day trip (*gita*) by train to Monza's Parco della Villa Reale: 10:12 train from Borgoverde, bike rental in the park, ducks at the laghetto, peanut-butter *merenda*, 17:40 train home. Kevin gets lost three times as official "navigatore" and mistakenly buys a dog ticket at the machine. Emma skips the trip, telling everyone she stayed home to study; she meets Tommaso and they skate in the piazza by the church canal.
- **New facts:** Ornella's cat is named Pavarotti. Tommaso plays bass (BASS sticker on his skateboard); Emma writes songs. Kevin's yellow notebook (holding plumber Signor Fontana's number) stayed on the kitchen table all day. Signor Sergio is a work contact of Chiara's; renovation workers (*muratori*) restart Monday at 7 a.m., Franco already there with the house plans.
- **Changed:** Ornella saw Emma leave and skate with Tommaso, and now knows she lied; she tells Emma she won't inform Chiara "per ora." Leo saw the dusty skateboard behind the garden wall; Emma hides it there.
- **Planted:** Ornella's silence and her watching window; Kevin alone at home with the workers Monday morning; Emma and Tommaso agreeing to meet Monday at the train.
