The Carters escape the dust of their renovation for a day at the park of the Villa Reale in Monza: train, rented bikes, a picnic, and ducks. Kevin is officially the navigator, which is a problem. Back on Via dei Tigli, Emma says she's staying home to study — but her skateboard and Tommaso have other plans, and Signora Ornella misses nothing from her window. Watch the ticket machine at the station: Kevin buys tickets for three travelers, and one of them is a dog.
