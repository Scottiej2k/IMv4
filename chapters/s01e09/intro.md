It’s all about questions in Borgoverde. Leo comes home with a sheet of ten of them, asking everyone from his teacher to Matteo at Bar Tigli to Nonno Franco among the tomatoes. At dinner, he turns them into a game for the whole family, while Kevin secretly fills a yellow notebook with Leo’s words. At her Milan studio, Chiara juggles English and Italian. And when Leo asks Franco where he goes on Sundays, the answer only opens a bigger question.
