### s03e04 · Come vi siete conosciuti?
- **Happened:** Emma (15) interviews Franco for a school research project by Prof.ssa Bassi on how her grandparents met; he tells it over two afternoons, then promises the wedding and lemon cake on Sunday.
- **Happened:** At Bar Tigli Matteo and Nadia tell Kevin their story; Kevin records both in his yellow notebook.
- **New facts:** Franco is 72. In 1975, aged 20, he drove Milan tram line 4 (San Siro–capolinea sud); Anna (18 in the photo) took it daily, 7:10 stop, always missed her stop reading. They spoke after two months; she waited at the capolinea with two coffees; first date at the ballo di San Giovanni in June; he said "mi sono innamorato" at a cinema in centro.
- **New facts:** Franco keeps a tin of photos; one of Anna at 18, which stays at his house. Nadia and Matteo met at her pharmacy: he faked illness in a white pharmacist's coat (gift from his aunt in Milan) and asked for throat cream; together six years; first date a four-hour dinner.
- **Changed:** Emma and Chiara learn the Anna–Franco story; Chiara had never heard the capolinea coffee or the ball. Lucia is now at Franco's house and knows everything; the family still doesn't know about her.
