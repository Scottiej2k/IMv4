Emma has a school assignment: interview an elderly couple about how they met. Simple—except her grandfather Franco never talks about her grandmother Anna, and his answer to every question is a shrug. Then, one afternoon in his kitchen on Via dei Tigli, he starts to tell a story about a tram, a girl who always missed her stop, and a tiny black-and-white photograph kept in a tin box. Will Emma's notebook finally unlock fifty years of silence?
