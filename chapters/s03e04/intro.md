Emma Carter needs an elderly couple for a school project on how people met. At home on Via dei Tigli, her grandfather Franco has never spoken about Grandma Anna—until Emma brings her notebook and a slice of apple. Slowly, he tells her about driving Milan’s number four tram in 1975, and a young woman with a book who always missed her stop. Then one day she stays on board. What happens when Franco calls out “Signorina, la sua fermata”? Meanwhile, at Bar Tigli, Matteo and Nadia argue over bouquets, white coats, and who is in love first.
