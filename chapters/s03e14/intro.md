It's Christmas Eve at the hospital in Monza, and the whole family is camped in the waiting room: Franco paces, Ornella knits, Kevin drinks terrible coffee, Leo fills his notebook with facts, Emma texts Bianca, and Samira sits quietly with a folded blanket. Nadia and Matteo are about to welcome their baby—a Christmas morning arrival that stirs up old promises and old grief. Watch for Leo’s certainty that a blue knitted blanket means a boy, and for the moment Franco hears the baby’s name and quietly leaves the room.
