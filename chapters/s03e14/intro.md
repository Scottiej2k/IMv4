It's Christmas Eve at the hospital in Monza, and the whole family is waiting: Nadia is having her baby. Franco paces the hallway, Leo fills his notebook with strange facts, Ornella knits something blue, and nobody can agree on what the baby should be called. There's coffee from the vending machine, snow outside, and one promise Franco made long ago. Then Nadia finally says the name — and Franco walks straight out of the room.
