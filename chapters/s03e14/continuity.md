### s03e14 · Benvenuta, Anna
- **Happened:** Nadia gives birth at Monza hospital on Christmas morning (4 a.m.) after a 12½-hour labor; the baby is a girl, 3.2 kg, black hair, Nadia's nose. The family names her **Anna Yasmin Ferri Benali** — Anna for Franco's late wife, Yasmin (jasmine) for Samira's mother, surname double. Franco walks out briefly, then returns; Kevin is floated as *padrino* ("forse"); Nadia decides on Ornella as *madrina*.
- **New facts:** Anna Yasmin Ferri Benali, born 25 December, year 2, daughter of Matteo and Nadia. Ornella's knitted blue blanket and two blue ribbons for the stroller; Samira's mother's Moroccan blanket. Matteo and Nadia plan to open Bar Tigli on 26 December to put up a "Benvenuta Anna Yasmin" sign. Leo's notebook of "fatti strani" reaches fact 32; Emma writes a theme on surnames; Kevin keeps a yellow notebook.
- **Changed:** Franco keeps a promise to Anna that every child carries a family name. Lucia is Franco's confidante in his grief. Tu/Lei: none.
- **Planted:** Kevin as *padrino* is only an idea; Ornella as *madrina* is Nadia's decision.
