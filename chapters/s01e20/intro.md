This week it’s all about Carnevale, and eight-year-old Leo has one big question: which costume? His American dad, Kevin, takes him to a shop in piazza della Chiesa, where sizes confuse everyone and a pirate outfit in red, green, or yellow waits to be tried on. Meanwhile, Emma swears she’s too old for masks at the Centro civico — until Tommaso starts filling boxes of coriandoli beside her. Watch for the moment Leo steps out of the changing room in a costume that’s just a little too long.
