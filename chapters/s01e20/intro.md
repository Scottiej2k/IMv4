It’s Carnevale week in Borgoverde, and Leo has one big problem: what will he wear to the school festival? Everyone has an opinion—red pirate, blue hero, black hat, even a skirt—but the real adventure happens at a costume shop with Kevin, where every choice brings a new disaster: too long, too tight, then the wrong color. Meanwhile, Emma sorts confetti at the community center and finds herself next to Tommaso. The line to listen for? “Questo o quello?”
