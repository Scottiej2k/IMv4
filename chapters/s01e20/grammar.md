# Questo and quello: "this" and "that"

## What they are

**Questo** means "this": something near you, or something you are talking about right now. **Quello** means "that": something far from you, or something you pointed at a moment ago.

Both words change with the noun they describe, like adjectives. **Questo** is regular and easy. **Quello** is a little special: in front of a noun it behaves like a definite article (*il*, *lo*, *la*, *i*, *gli*, *le*), so it changes shape depending on how the next word begins.

You can also use both words alone, with no noun after them, as pronouns: *Questo è bello.* ("This one is nice.") *Quello no.* ("That one isn't.")

## How it works

**Questo** follows the noun ending.

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **questo** costume | **questi** costumi |
| masculine before a vowel | **quest'**amico | **questi** amici |
| feminine | **questa** maglia | **queste** maglie |

**Quello** in front of a noun follows the pattern of the article.

| the noun starts with… | singular | plural | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| a consonant | **quel** | **quei** | **quel** cappello, **quei** pantaloni |
| s + consonant, z, gn, ps, x | **quello** | **quegli** | **quello** specchio, **quegli** specchi |
| a vowel | **quell'** | **quegli** | **quell'**uomo, **quegli** uomini |
| feminine | **quella** | **quelle** | **quella** gonna, **quelle** scarpe |
| feminine before a vowel | **quell'** | **quelle** | **quell'**amica, **quelle** amiche |

As pronouns, **quello** has just four forms: **quello** (that one, m. sing.), **quella** (f. sing.), **quelli** (m. pl.), **quelle** (f. pl.).

The idea behind both words is distance: **questo** here, **quello** there. If you are holding the hat, it's *questo cappello*. If the hat is on the other side of the room, it's *quel cappello*.

## From the story

«Sì, Leo. **Questo** o **quello**.» — “Yes, Leo. **This one** or **that one**.” (`s01e20-2-055`)
Kevin offers Leo the two choices with the two pronouns, and the shop window (far away) will decide.

«**Quello** è un supereroe.» — “**That one** is a superhero.” (`s01e20-2-051`)
*Quello* as a pronoun, for a costume that is over there on the wall.

«E **questo** è un gatto.» — “And **this one** is a cat.” (`s01e20-2-052`)
*Questo* as a pronoun, for the costume the shop assistant is touching.

«**Quella** **vetrina** in piazza è cara.» — “**That** **shop window** in the piazza is expensive.” (`s01e20-3-032`)
*Quella* before a feminine noun: the article pattern of *quello*.

«**Questa** **maglia** è a righe!» — “**This** **sweater** has stripes!” (`s01e20-2-063`)
Feminine singular agreement: *maglia* is feminine, so *questa*.

«**Questi** sono i miei preferiti.» — “**These** are my favorites.” (`s01e20-4-034`)
Masculine plural pronoun: *questi*, a handful of confetti in Emma's hand.

«Il **rosso** è un **colore** bello, ma **questa** **maschera** è **brutta**.» — “**Red** is a nice **color**, but **this** **mask** is **ugly**.” (`s01e20-4-062`)
*Questo* used a second time, pointing at the mask right in front of her.

«**Questo** **cappello** è di mio nonno.» — “**This** **hat** was my grandfather's.” (`s01e20-3-062`)
In front of a noun, with a possessive after it.

«**Questo** è bello.» — “**This** is nice.” (`s01e20-4-090`)
Here *questo* is a pronoun: no noun follows it.

## Common mistakes

- **Saying *questo maglia*.** *Maglia* and *gonna* are feminine: *questa maglia*, *questa gonna*. Always match the ending of the noun.
- **Using *quello cappello*.** Before a normal consonant, *quello* becomes *quel*: *quel cappello*, *quel negozio*.
- **Forgetting the plural.** English "these" and "those" are one word each. Italian has *questi/queste* and *quei/quegli/quelle*, and they must agree with the noun.
- **Mixing up *quel* and *quello*.** The long form *quello* comes before s + consonant, z and gn: *quello specchio*, *quello studente*. The short form *quel* is for everything else: *quel ragazzo*.
- **Translating "this" and "that" word for word.** In Italian, *questo* is also used for something you are about to name, where English might say "it".

## Practice

Fill in the blank with the right form of **questo** or **quello**.

1. ______ scarpe sono nere. (these)
2. ______ cappello in vetrina è caro. (that)
3. ______ maglia è nuova. (this)
4. ______ costume è di mio nonno. (this)
5. ______ pantaloni sono lunghi. (those)
6. ______ gonna è brutta. (that)
7. ______ specchi sono vecchi. (those, before s + consonant)
8. ______ è il colore giusto? (this one — pronoun)

### Answer key

1. **Queste** scarpe sono nere.
2. **Quel** cappello in vetrina è caro.
3. **Questa** maglia è nuova.
4. **Questo** costume è di mio nonno.
5. **Quei** pantaloni sono lunghi.
6. **Quella** gonna è brutta.
7. **Quegli** specchi sono vecchi.
8. **Questo** è il colore giusto?
