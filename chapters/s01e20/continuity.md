### s01e20 · Questo o quello?
- **Happened:** Leo needs a Carnival costume for the school party on Friday; Kevin takes him to a clothing shop on Piazza della Chiesa, where he learns *taglia* (clothes) vs *numero* (shoes). After trying sizes too long and too tight, Leo picks a red pirate costume with black-and-white striped shirt; Kevin pays €25 (costume €20 + black hat €5). Lucia takes the costume home to shorten it with her old sewing machine; Leo wears it to the school parade Friday, where Franco watches from the wall.

Leo's costume: red pirate, striped black-and-white shirt, black pants, black hat, black shoes. Maestra Paola teaches Leo's class. Franco's black hat is his grandfather's and was his tram hat for forty years. Carnival festival for teens at the Centro civico (Tuesday); school Rodari party Friday.
