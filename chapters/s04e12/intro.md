Kevin has big plans for his very first cooking class for foreigners at Bar Tigli: three paying students, a chalkboard full of rules, and one pot of tomato sauce that has to be perfect. But between a German note-taker, an engineer with a bag of turmeric, and a shy American student, his grandfather-in-law Franco keeps appearing outside under the awning, insisting he is only "passing by." When Franco finally tastes a plate of bright yellow pasta, will the toughest critic on Via dei Tigli say more than he usually does?
