Leo has invented a game: a notebook where he scores what everyone likes. At Sunday lunch his mom, Chiara, says “a me no” to every dish Lucia serves — even the ones she secretly loves. Across the street, Kevin cooks an American dinner for the Colombo family, hoping to win over grumpy Roberto, who dislikes everything. Watch for Marina, Roberto's quiet wife, who waits until the door to whisper a secret that would horrify her husband.
