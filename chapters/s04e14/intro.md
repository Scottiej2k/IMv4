This week Chiara is in Rome, commuting between her family in Borgoverde and a demanding new project. Meanwhile, a phone call offers Chiara something bigger than she expected, and a late-night bowl of soup forces one honest question. Watch for Leo's fourth rule, and for the moment Franco tastes the onions and delivers a verdict that stops Kevin cold.
