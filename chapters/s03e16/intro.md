Moving day on Via dei Tigli: Lucia's boxes fill Franco's hallway, and Franco can't bear to move Anna's things, not even the drawer of her napkins. Kevin, notebook in hand, decides to fare spazio. Across the street, Emma and Leo divide a brand-new room and argue over every chair — until Leo claims the middle shelf for his stones, three and a half of them. All weekend everyone asks the same question: is it mine or yours? The answer turns up on the shelf, fast asleep.
