### s03e23 · Che bello!
- **Happened:** Carnevale Saturday: Kevin burns 12 chiacchiere, then buys 30; the family joins the Milan parade. Franco, in a cardboard Tram 4 costume built with Lucia, is applauded, wins the costume contest judged by children, and gives the chocolate prize to Leo.
- **New facts:** Franco drove Milan tram 4 to San Siro and knows its capolinea; his green-and-white cardboard tram reads CAPOLINEA — TRAM 4, wheels by Lucia. Leo and Pietro wrote a four-panel fumetto; Pietro’s cat is Nerone. Maestra Paola teaches Leo’s class, quarta B (24 pupils). A Milan pasticceria sells a chocolate cat for €8.
- **Changed:** Franco enjoys public recognition and applause despite embarrassment. Emma secretly keeps a photo of Franco and doesn’t share it.
- **Planted:** Leo and Pietro plan a second fumetto about school; Leo wants to buy the chocolate cat; Emma may write a real fumetto. Emma hoped Tommaso would appear in Milan (he doesn’t). Franco says he won’t do a costume next year.
