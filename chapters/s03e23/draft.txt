# Exclamations and interjections: *Che bello!*

## What it is

Italian loves a reaction. Where English says "How nice!" or "That's great!", Italian mostly uses one small word: **che**. Add an adjective or a noun and you have an exclamation:

- **Che bello!** — How lovely!
- **Che peccato!** — What a shame!
- **Che schifo!** — How gross!

There is also a second family: short words you say *instead of* a full sentence. These are **interjections**: **dai!**, **magari!**, **figurati!**, **accidenti!**, **uffa!**, **boh**. They carry the emotion, and the grammar stays out of the way.

## How it works

### 1. *Che* + adjective or noun

| Structure | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| che + adjective | **Che bello!** / **Che stupendo!** | How lovely! / How wonderful! |
| che + noun | **Che peccato!** / **Che vergogna!** | What a shame! / How embarrassing! |
| che bello + a / di + infinitive | **Che bello vedere il tram!** | How lovely to see the tram! |

**Che** never changes: *che bello, che bella, che belli, che belle* all keep **che**. The adjective agrees with the thing you are talking about:

- **Che bello** il tram!
- **Che bella** la lumaca!
- **Che belli** i coriandoli!

### 2. *Come* and *quanto* + verb

Use **come** before a verb to mean "how …!":

- **Come sei bravo!** — How good you are!
- **Come parla bene!** — How well he speaks!

Use **quanto/quanta/quanti/quante** before a noun to mean "what a lot of …!" — and here it does agree:

- **Quanto** pubblico!
- **Quanta** folla!
- **Quanti** coriandoli!

### 3. Interjections

| Word | When you say it |
|---|---|
| **dai!** | to push someone: "come on!" |
| **magari!** | "I wish!" / "maybe!" |
| **figurati!** | "don't mention it!", "no problem!" |
| **accidenti!** | surprise or annoyance: "wow!", "darn!" |
| **uffa!** | tiredness, boredom: "ugh!" |
| **boh** | "I have no idea." |
| **mah / bah** | doubt: "well…", "hmm." |

**Dai** and **uffa** are spoken, not written, Italian. **Figurati** is the *tu* form; with a stranger you say **si figuri**.

## Common mistakes

- **"Che è bello!"** — wrong. Italian drops the verb: **Che bello!**
- **"Quanto bello!"** — wrong. Use **che** + adjective, **quanto** + noun.
- **"Come è bello!"** — understandable but not idiomatic. Say **Che bello!**
- Making **che** agree: *"Che bella!"* is correct only if the thing is feminine. Before a verb or on its own, it's always **che bello**.
- Translating **magari** only as "maybe". It often means **"I wish!"**: *"Vieni a Milano?" "Magari!"*
- Translating **figurati** literally ("imagine yourself"). It means "no problem", "don't mention it".
- Using **dai** with a stranger. With *Lei*, use **prego** or **si figuri**.
- Overusing **uffa** in writing. It's fine in a comic, in a message, in speech — rare in a formal text.
