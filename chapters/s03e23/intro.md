It's carnival time in Milan, and the Carter family is heading into the city for the big parade. Kevin has baked a tray of very dark, very crunchy pastries, Leo is carrying a homemade comic, and Emma is taking secret photos. But the real star is Grandpa Franco, who steps out of his front door in a homemade cardboard tram, complete with a sign reading TRAM 4. Soon a crowd of strangers is shouting "Il quattro!"—and somehow Franco ends up in a costume contest judged by three children. What could possibly go wrong?
