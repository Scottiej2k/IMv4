It's Christmas in Via dei Tigli, and Kevin is determined to do everything the American way — except the tree barely fits through the front door, and there's a family *presepe* to fit in too. Meanwhile, Franco asks Kevin to keep one small secret about a mysterious guest at Christmas Eve dinner, a job that Kevin is far too proud of. Then Kevin hides the presents somewhere very safe, forgets where, and the whole family starts hunting — while the neighbor's cat keeps quietly moving things around under the sofa.
