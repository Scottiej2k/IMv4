Kevin Carter has one big job today: the residency form at the Borgoverde town hall. Chiara coaches him on phone numbers, addresses and birth dates, while Emma needs to add her number to the class WhatsApp group at school. At the office, one number keeps following Kevin around — “diciassette” — and he scribbles it three times in his yellow notebook, sure it’s the hardest word in Italy. Will the right counter ever find him?
