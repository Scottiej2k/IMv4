Kevin has a project: a notebook of Italian rules for his cookbook, from cappuccino hours to elbows on the table. Chiara, Franco and Ornella all agree they're not quite right — while Leo, eight years old and serious, decides to test every single rule himself around town. That's how he ends up at Bar Tigli at noon, ordering a large cappuccino, with a counter full of customers watching. Later, the family dinner at Franco's place goes ahead, where Kevin has one more question nobody expects.
