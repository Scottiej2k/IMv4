Kevin’s Monday starts with big plans for a new kitchen, but by lunchtime the builders have opened the wrong wall, the water is off, and the floor is wet. While Chiara waits for calm answers, Kevin takes photos, calls a plumber, and starts his own investigation. Across the street, Roberto Colombo writes down every fact in his notebook. Then a mysterious red pencil arrow appears on a drawing — a treasure map, someone says, with a neighborhood cat as the explorer. Who drew it, and why?
