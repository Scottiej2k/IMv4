This week the Carters' kitchen renovation turns into a comedy of errors. Kevin is in charge while Chiara works, but the builders open the wrong wall, the water stops, and a pipe starts leaking. Down the street, Roberto Colombo records every leak and noise in his notebook. Meanwhile, Leo has a secret plan involving a cat named Pavarotti. When Kevin finds a red pencil arrow on his kitchen drawing, he launches a full investigation — and the answer is not what he expects.
