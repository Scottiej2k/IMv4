It's Kevin's big day out: he's taking Emma and Leo from Borgoverde to Milan, with a list for everything — tickets, platform, timetable. Leo brings the camera; Emma would rather not be seen with her American dad at all. Between a stubborn ticket machine and a metro ride in the wrong direction, the city still works its magic. Watch for the moment on the train when Emma calls Kevin 'un amico di famiglia' in front of schoolmates — and Kevin stands up to introduce himself to the whole car.
