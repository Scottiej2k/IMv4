Kevin Carter has a new idea: a cooking course for foreigners at Bar Tigli, and it starts Saturday. The problem is that Kevin has never taught anyone anything, so first he practices on his own family, who sit on bar stools pretending to be beginners from other countries. Between Franco's proverbs, Emma's corrections and a yellow notebook full of doubts, Kevin learns what teaching really takes. Watch for the moment he stirs the pot with the wooden spoon the wrong way around — and for who smells his mistakes before he does.
