It's Sunday on Via dei Tigli, and Franco's family and neighbors are gathering at number nine for his seventy-second birthday. Kevin is determined to give the perfect toast, written out on a yellow card that keeps vanishing into his shirt pocket. Emma nervously invites Tommaso to lunch, Chiara carries Anna's tiramisù to the table, and committee president Roberto wants to know whether this party has an official "verbale." Franco says he doesn't talk much — but on this birthday, he has something to say.
