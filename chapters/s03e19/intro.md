Franco has noisy knees, a stubborn streak, and zero interest in seeing a doctor. In Borgoverde, he blames the creaking on Kevin’s very American bocce throw, while Lucia, Chiara, and Nadia secretly form an "alleanza" to get him checked. Meanwhile, Leo celebrates his newly cast-free arm with X-rays and hospital stories, and Kevin somehow lands inside the women’s plot. Watch for Franco’s one unbreakable condition: a promise about injections.
