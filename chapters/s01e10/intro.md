Kevin Carter is nervous about his first Italian lesson at the Centro civico in Borgoverde. He packs a notebook, two pens, and one apple for the teacher — then worries all through class about the homework due Tuesday. Meanwhile, Emma gets a 5 on her Italian test at school in Monza, and surprising help arrives from the boy whose grade is the best in class. Back at the community center, Kevin opens the wrong door during the break and finds his father-in-law Franco in a tango lesson with a woman named Lucia. Franco has just one word for him: “Nessuno sa.”
