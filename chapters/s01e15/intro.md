It's early January in Borgoverde, and Kevin Carter is determined to organize his family's new year with a giant calendar and a yellow notebook. Unfortunately, his notes keep changing: Emma's dentist appointment appears three times, and Kevin's own calendar contradicts itself. Meanwhile, Leo is convinced the Befana will visit on January 6, and Grandpa Franco's house seems to be expecting someone special. Watch for a red scarf left on a chair—and for Leo's very direct question.
