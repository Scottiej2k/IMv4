Chiara is away in Rome all week, and Kevin is alone with Emma, Leo, the bar, eight deliveries a day and a new American breakfast to perfect. He insists he’s fine — “me la cavo,” he keeps saying — but he sleeps four hours a night, buys soy milk by mistake, and by Wednesday something has to give. When he can’t stand his own empty kitchen any longer, he slips out late at night and finds one light still on down the street: Franco’s. What his father-in-law finally says, among the tomato plants, is not at all what Kevin expects.
