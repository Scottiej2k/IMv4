# The imperfetto for description: *com'era la strada*

## What it is

The **imperfetto** is a past tense. You use it here for one job: **describing how things were** in the past — a place, a person, the weather, the time, a feeling.

It does not say "this happened." It says "this is what things were like."

- *La strada **era** stretta.* — The street **was** narrow.
- *Qui **c'erano** i campi.* — Here there **were** the fields.
- *Gino **aveva** ventotto anni.* — Gino **was** twenty-eight.
- *In inverno **faceva freddo**.* — In winter **it was cold**.

Think of it as a photograph, not a video: nothing moves, you are simply looking at the scene.

## How it works

### The forms

Most verbs are regular. Take the stem and add the ending:

| | parlare | credere | dormire |
|---|---|---|---|
| io | parlavo | credevo | dormivo |
| tu | parlavi | credevi | dormivi |
| lui / lei | parlava | credeva | dormiva |
| noi | parlavamo | credevamo | dormivamo |
| voi | parlavate | credevate | dormivate |
| loro | parlavano | credevano | dormivano |

**-are** verbs take *-avo, -avi, -ava, -avamo, -avate, -avano*. **-ere** and **-ire** verbs take *-evo/-ivo*, and so on. Note that *io* and *lui/lei* forms look nothing like the present: *parlo* → *parlavo*, *dorme* → *dormiva*.

Only four verbs are irregular, and you will see them all the time:

| | essere | avere | fare | dire |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| io | ero | avevo | facevo | dicevo |
| tu | eri | avevi | facevi | dicevi |
| lui / lei | era | aveva | faceva | diceva |
| noi | eravamo | avevamo | facevamo | dicevamo |
| voi | eravate | avevate | facevate | dicevate |
| loro | erano | avevano | facevano | dicevano |

### *C'era* and *c'erano*

To describe what existed somewhere, use **c'era** (one thing) or **c'erano** (more than one). They are *ci* + the imperfetto of *essere*.

- *C'era una latteria piccola.* — There was a small dairy shop.
- *C'erano due mucche e un cavallo.* — There were two cows and a horse.

The rule is the same as in the present (*c'è* / *ci sono*): **c'era** with a singular noun, **c'erano** with a plural noun.

### Describing weather and feelings

Weather is very often imperfetto: *faceva freddo* (it was cold), *faceva caldo* (it was hot), *c'era nebbia* (there was fog), *pioveva* (it was raining). Feelings and states too: *ero stanco* (I was tired), *avevo paura* (I was afraid), *volevo un caffè* (I wanted a coffee).

## Common mistakes

- **Using the passato prossimo to describe.** *La strada è stata stretta* does not mean "the street was narrow." It means "the street was narrow for a while / at some point." For a description, use *era*.
- **Mixing up *c'era* and *c'erano*.** *C'era due mucche* is wrong; plural needs *c'erano*.
- **Forgetting that *io* and *tu* take -s.** English speakers often say *io era* or *io ero* correctly but then *lui ero*. *Lui/lei* is always **era**.
- **Treating *gente* as plural.** *La gente* is singular in Italian: *la gente era poca*, never *la gente erano poche*.
- **Saying *faceva freddo* with *essere*.** It is *faceva freddo* (from *fare*), not *era freddo* — that describes an object being cold.
- **Using the imperfetto for a single finished event.** *Ieri ho aperto il bar alle sei* (a real event), not *aprii/aprivo*.

## Practice

1. Complete with *c'era* or *c'erano*: Nel 1962 ______ solo tre case.
2. Complete with the imperfetto of *essere*: La strada ______ stretta e senza marciapiede.
3. Complete with the imperfetto of *avere*: Mio nonno ______ ventotto anni.
4. Complete with the imperfetto of *fare*: In inverno ______ freddo.
5. Complete with the imperfetto of *vivere*: Nella cascina ______ dieci persone.
6. Rewrite in the imperfetto: *Oggi c'è nebbia.* → *Ieri ______ nebbia.*
7. Fix the mistake: *La gente erano poche, ma si conosceva tutta.*
8. Describe your street ten years ago in two sentences, using *era* and *c'era / c'erano*.

2. **era**
