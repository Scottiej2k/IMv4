Kevin has a new job in Via dei Tigli: he's the neighborhood's official writer. When Signora Ornella refuses to be interviewed about the street's past for the Christmas newsletter, she names Kevin instead, and he sets out with his yellow notebook to find out what Via dei Tigli looked like in 1962 — cows, chickens, a mill, one single lamppost. Leo cannot believe any of it. Meanwhile, in Franco's hallway, Chiara counts seven boxes and says nothing.
