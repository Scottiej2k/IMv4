Nobody asked Kevin Carter to become the neighborhood historian — but Ornella has volunteered him anyway. Roberto wants a page about old Via dei Tigli for the committee's Christmas newsletter, written in Italian, and Kevin isn't sure he's the man for it. Luckily, Leo can't stop asking questions about the old days: no phones, no sidewalk, and cows in the courtyard of a Milan suburb. Roberto wants dates and numbers. Kevin just wants to make his neighbors proud. And readers should keep an eye out for the cows.
