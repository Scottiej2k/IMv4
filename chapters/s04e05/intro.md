It's wedding season on Via dei Tigli, and Franco and Lucia are trying to plan a simple June ceremony. Everyone has an opinion: Ornella insists on five sugared almonds ("confetti") and an odd number, Samira wants a huge party, Emma suggests Instagram, and Kevin dreams up a cooking course and American brunch. Amid the chaos, the family counts almonds, argues about a toast, and waits for Franco to name his best man. Then Leo quietly eats nine of them—a perfect odd number, he argues—and Kevin has to explain that to Ornella.
