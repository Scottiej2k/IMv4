It's the Carters' last weekend on Via dei Tigli, and Kevin's American parents, Doug and Linda, are squeezing a mountain of gifts into three suitcases while Leo weighs everything on the bathroom scale and announces a world record. Farewell gifts turn up everywhere: Franco's kitchen, Ornella's living room, the tables at Bar Tigli. Then watch Franco's hallway, where he hands Doug his old blue tram-driver's cap. Kevin says it's a loan — and Franco very firmly says it's something else.
