# Mentre and the Imperfect Progressive

## What it is

Italian has two very natural ways to say what was going on when something happened:

1. **mentre** + imperfect — *mentre cucinavo, è arrivato Marco* ("while I was cooking, Marco arrived")
2. **stare** + gerund — *stavo cucinando* ("I was cooking")

They often work together: *mentre dormivo* and *stavo dormendo* both mean "while I was sleeping / I was sleeping." English uses the same idea with "-ing," so once you know the forms, this is one of the friendliest structures at A2.

## How it works

### Mentre

*Mentre* means "while." It links two things happening at the same time. Both verbs are usually in the **imperfetto** (or both in the present).

| Italian | English |
|---|---|
| **Mentre** mescolavo, ero sulla porta. | **While** I was stirring, I was at the door. |
| **Mentre** Leo cadeva, Tommaso correva verso di lui. | **While** Leo was falling, Tommaso was running toward him. |
| **Mentre** dormi, Leo sogna lo skateboard. | **While** you sleep, Leo dreams about the skateboard. |

Use *mentre* for **background**, not for a single finished event. For "and then suddenly," you switch to the passato prossimo: *Mentre cucinavo, è arrivato Marco.*

### Stare + gerund

This is the "progressive": it shows an action **in progress** at that moment.

| Person | Form |
|---|---|
| io | stavo + -ando / -endo |
| tu | stavi + gerund |
| lui/lei | stava + gerund |
| noi | stavamo + gerund |
| voi | stavate + gerund |
| loro | stavano + gerund |

Gerund forms: **-are** → *-ando* (parlando, mescolando, guardando); **-ere** / **-ire** → *-endo* (correndo, dormendo, facendo).

- **Io stavo facendo** un caffè americano.
- **Stavo guardando** la ruota davanti.
- **Stavamo parlando** del colore del legno.

Irregular gerunds you'll meet: **facendo** (fare), **dicendo** (dire), **bevendo** (bere).

### The two together

Very common and very natural:

- **Mentre Leo andava, io stavo guardando** la ruota davanti.
- **Mentre tu stavi facendo** il caffè, io preparavo questo.

You can also ask about it: *Che cosa stavi facendo?*

## From the story

«**Mentre** mescolavo, ero sulla porta.» — "**While** I was stirring, I was at the door." (`s03e05-2-074`)
Two actions going on at once, both in the imperfetto.

«Io **stavo facendo** un caffè americano.» — "I **was making** an American coffee." (`s03e05-2-073`)
*Stare* + gerund: the classic "I was making."

«**Mentre** Leo **cadeva**, Tommaso **correva** verso di lui.» — "**While** Leo **was falling**, Tommaso **was running** toward him." (`s03e05-3-043`)
*Mentre* with two imperfects: background and simultaneous action.

«**Stavo guardando** la ruota davanti.» — "**I was watching** the front wheel." (`s03e05-2-086`)
The progressive on its own, without *mentre*.

«**Mentre** Leo **andava**, io **stavo guardando** la ruota davanti.» — "**While** Leo **was going**, I **was watching** the front wheel." (`s03e05-5-024`)
The two structures combined in one sentence.

«Che cosa **stavate facendo** voi?» — "What **were you all doing**?" (`s03e05-4-027`)
Question form of the progressive: *voi* → *stavate*.

«**Mentre** preparo l'**ingessatura**, raccontatemi di nuovo.» — "**While** I get the **cast** ready, tell me again." (`s03e05-4-026`)
With *mentre*, the present also works, for things happening now.

«Per il paziente,» dice Matteo. «**Mentre** tu **stavi facendo** il caffè, io **preparavo** questo.» — "For the patient," says Matteo. "**While** you **were making** coffee, I **was making** this." (`s03e05-6-064`)
Two people, two parallel actions, one *mentre*.

«**Mentre** dormi, Leo sogna lo skateboard.» — "**While** you sleep, Leo dreams about the skateboard." (`s03e05-5-056`)
*Mentre* + present, for a repeated or general situation.

## Common mistakes

- **Don't use passato prossimo after *mentre* for the background.** *Mentre ho cucinato* is wrong; say *Mentre cucinavo*.
- **Don't mix gerunds wrong.** -are verbs take **-ando** (*parlando*), not *-endo*.
- **Don't add a preposition.** Say *stavo guardando*, never *stavo a guardando*.
- **Gerund is not the same as English -ing everywhere.** After *mentre*, Italian often prefers the plain imperfect: *mentre dormivo* is more common than *mentre stavo dormendo* — both are fine, but the simple one is lighter.
- **Stare is not essere.** *Ero guardando* is not Italian. Use *stavo guardando*.
- **Watch the time of day.** *Mentre* connects actions in the same time frame; if one clearly interrupts and ends the other, use passato prossimo for the second: *Mentre leggevo, è suonato il telefono.*

## Practice

Translate or complete.

1. While I was cooking, my brother arrived. (start with *Mentre*)
2. I was writing in the yellow notebook when Chiara called. (*stavo*…)
3. Mentre Leo ______ (cadere), Tommaso ______ (correre) verso di lui.
4. Che cosa ______ (tu / stare / fare) ieri alle sette?
5. While you sleep, the cast stays on. (*mentre*)
6. Noi ______ (stare / parlare) del colore del legno.
7. Mentre io ______ (mescolare) il caffè, il cane ha abbaiato.
8. Complete: *Mentre ______ (noi / dormire), è arrivata una telefonata.*

### Answer key

1. Mentre cucinavo, è arrivato mio fratello.
2. Stavo scrivendo sul quaderno giallo quando Chiara ha chiamato.
3. Mentre Leo cadeva, Tommaso correva verso di lui.
4. Che cosa stavi facendo ieri alle sette?
5. Mentre dormi, l'ingessatura resta.
6. Noi stavamo parlando del colore del legno.
7. Mentre io mescolavo il caffè, il cane ha abbaiato.
8. Mentre dormivamo, è arrivata una telefonata.
