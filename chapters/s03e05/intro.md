It's a busy Saturday on Via dei Tigli: eight-year-old Leo wants a turn on Tommaso's skateboard, and everyone watches — Emma filming with her phone, Dad making his American coffee. Then Leo falls, and his arm is all wrong. By the time the family reaches the hospital in Monza, the whole street knows, and Roberto insists it's Tommaso's fault. Kevin, to everyone's surprise, defends the boy. Watch for the moment Roberto signs Leo's white cast and adds one unexpected word: “pazienza.”
