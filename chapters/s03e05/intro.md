It’s Saturday afternoon on Via dei Tigli, and eight-year-old Leo wants one turn on Tommaso’s skateboard. One small stone later, Leo is on his way to the hospital in Monza with a strange-looking arm, while Emma has the whole fall on video. At the hospital, Roberto Colombo arrives and loudly blames Tommaso — until Kevin steps in. Watch for the next morning at Bar Tigli, when Pavarotti the cat chooses Leo’s new white cast as his personal bed, and Roberto asks to sign it with one surprising word: “pazienza.”
