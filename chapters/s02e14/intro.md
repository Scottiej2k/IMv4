Kevin’s new oven is broken, and his week becomes a battle with customer service: endless hold music, long queues, a missing receipt, and a code he keeps getting wrong. Meanwhile, Chiara’s patience breaks at work and at her father’s kitchen table, where Franco offers an old recipe and a little calm. Warm and funny, this episode is about waiting for repairs—and for each other. Watch for eight-year-old Leo, who proves that “niente” is still something.
