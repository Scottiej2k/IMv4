It's tiramisù week at Bar Tigli. Matteo announces a contest: three bakers, one judge, and a silver plaque for the best dessert on Via dei Tigli. Lucia bakes from Anna's old recipe, Samira brings her own, and Kevin enters with a secret American twist. Franco, the judge, takes his job very seriously—and the contest becomes something warmer than anyone expected. Watch for the moment Franco sniffs Kevin's tiramisù like a truffle-hunting dog.
