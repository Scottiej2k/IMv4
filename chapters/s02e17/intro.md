It's tiramisù week at Bar Tigli. Matteo stages a contest, with Franco as judge and Nadia and Ornella on the jury. Kevin enters a secret American version—peanut butter included—while Lucia cooks from Anna's handwritten recipe. As the neighborhood gathers, old memories and new flavors collide. Watch for Kevin's secret ingredient and for Ornella's one-word verdict, "discreto," which may not mean what he hopes.
