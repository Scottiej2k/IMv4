Nadia has news for Matteo: she's three months pregnant. But it's Sunday afternoon, the Milan derby is on, and Bar Tigli is full of shouting fans, so she waits for a quiet moment. Meanwhile, the whole street already seems to know the secret, from Ornella to little Leo, and nobody is supposed to say a word. Watch for the moment Nadia finally tells him, mid-match, when a cup slips from Matteo's hand, and for a supermarket cart that keeps turning up outside the Carters' house.
