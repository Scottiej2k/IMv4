Kevin is behind the Bar Tigli counter, two months into the job, when Matteo spots a dream job at a food company in Milan. The catch: Kevin must write his résumé in Italian, with Emma correcting every line, then survive an interview and a very tempting offer. As Franco, Nadia, and Matteo each weigh in, Ornella offers one piece of advice: "Dormici sopra." Will Kevin know what he really wants by morning?
