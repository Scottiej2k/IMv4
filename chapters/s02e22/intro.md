It's August in Borgoverde, and the Carter family has two problems: no ice cream anywhere, and no idea where to go on vacation. Kevin wants the sea, Chiara wants the mountains, Emma wants to stay home with her friends, and Leo wants all three plus a giant cone. Meanwhile, someone is eating every "gelato" on the street, and the prime suspect is Ornella's cat. Watch for Leo's backpack: inside there's sunscreen, a rock for the neighbor, and a photo of that cat.
