It's Easter Monday, and Kevin has planned the perfect lakeside picnic for thirteen people, two cars, and a whole lot of food. Chiara counts the baskets, Franco predicts the wind to the hour, Nadia's mother watches her daughter not eating, and Roberto wants a form signed. Somewhere in the middle of it all, Kevin discovers an extra chocolate egg hidden in the woods — one he hid himself and then forgot. Find out what everyone says about his picnic.
