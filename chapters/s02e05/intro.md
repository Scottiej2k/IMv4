Easter at Via dei Tigli 14, and Kevin is up at dawn hiding chocolate eggs, flipping pancakes and promising the perfect lake picnic for thirteen people, two cars and a picnic basket of his own. Around him, Nadia dodges her mother's questions, Matteo dodges the notary, and Franco dodges nothing but the wind. Somewhere in the middle of all of it, Kevin discovers a sixth chocolate egg, still wrapped, in a place nobody remembers hiding it.
