It's Saturday on Via dei Tigli, and Emma wants to go out with friends — but Mom demands names, a place, and a midnight curfew. Meanwhile, Kevin accepts a guys' night out with Matteo, who thinks he knows all the rules of an Italian evening. At the pizzeria, Kevin orders something that makes the whole room go silent — a cappuccino. As Emma's mysterious "compagnia" finally takes shape, Franco arrives in his good jacket to settle the family argument.
