It's Saturday in Via dei Tigli, and two Carters want to go out. Emma asks her parents for permission to spend the evening with friends — but Mom has three conditions, and one of them is midnight. Meanwhile, Chiara's brother Matteo invites Kevin to a men's night at the pizzeria, where Kevin is sure he knows the rules. He doesn't. Watch the whole restaurant go quiet when he orders a "cappuccino" with his pizza — and find out whether Emma gets home on time.
