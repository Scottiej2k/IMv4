Winter has come to Borgoverde, and Nadia decides it's finally time to look at Bar Tigli's books. Matteo isn't thrilled. She's on maternity leave, she has time, and the numbers, she says, are not growing. Then, tucked deep inside the account notebook, she finds a folded piece of paper with four small words on it: «Franco. Trentamila. Nessun interesse». What has Matteo kept hidden for two years? And what happens when the truth reaches Franco's kitchen table?
