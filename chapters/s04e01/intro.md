At Franco and Lucia's engagement lunch on Via dei Tigli, the whole family has opinions about the wedding—except about the two people getting married. Franco wants a quick civil ceremony, Lucia dreams of church flowers and a tango, and Chiara arrives with a serious list of three dates. Meanwhile, Kevin keeps trying to announce his new business, a breakfast delivery service called “Buongiorno, Borgoverde,” but every time he opens his yellow notebook, someone asks for bread or wine. Watch for Leo’s wedding-cake idea: a cake shaped like a tram.
