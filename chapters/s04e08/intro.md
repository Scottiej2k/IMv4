This week, two quiet confessions change everything on Via dei Tigli. Chiara visits her father's garden, where Gennaro finally talks about his mother Lucia's long-ago divorce and the lonely years she never mentioned. Meanwhile, after closing time at the pharmacy, Nadia tells Matteo about a job offer in Milan she has kept secret for seven months. Through it all, Kevin keeps his yellow notebook and his opinions. Then Gennaro's suitcase finally arrives, sixteen days late, and inside is a photograph no one has ever mentioned.
