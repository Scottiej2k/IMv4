Kevin has a plan: a family day in Milan, a yellow notebook, and an official role as tour guide. There's just one problem — nobody follows quite the way he expects. His mom disappears into shop windows, his dad would rather stay home, and the whole trip ends up somewhere unexpected involving a bridge, a canal, and no panorama at all. Meanwhile, back on Via dei Tigli, something even stranger happens: Franco and Doug discover they get along without a single shared language. Watch for the moment when the family realizes exactly who the real guide is.
