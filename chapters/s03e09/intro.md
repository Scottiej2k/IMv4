Kevin is determined to be his family's official guide for a day in Milan: Duomo, Galleria, Castello, all in one trip. He has a yellow notebook, a hat, and a plan. His kids are skeptical, his mother is distracted by shop windows, and his father, Doug, suddenly claims he's tired — until Franco arrives with tomatoes and basil. Before the Carters reach the Duomo, something unexpected happens in Franco's garden. Watch for one small word, “Bocce,” that bridges two languages and two stubborn grandfathers.
