This week the story belongs to Franco. His kitchen is a mess—drawers open, old papers everywhere—because Anna's gold wedding ring, the one he gave her in 1978, is missing. He asks Chiara, "L'hai vista?" while Kevin declares himself manager of the search and Leo asks why things disappear at all. At Bar Tigli, Matteo has lost three parenting manuals; on Via dei Tigli, the neighborhood cat, Pavarotti, has vanished too. Just wait until you see where Pavarotti turns up—and why.
