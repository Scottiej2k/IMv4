Kevin Carter shows up at Bar Tigli in his best blue jacket, ready for a job interview. Only problem: there is no interview — just a green apron, a tray of cups, and a very early shift. As Kevin learns what a real *barista* does, Chiara worries about what her elegant colleagues in Milan will think, Nadia needs a morning off, and Franco insists on waiting in line like everybody else. Watch for a coffee receipt from 2019 and one stubborn "Mah.
