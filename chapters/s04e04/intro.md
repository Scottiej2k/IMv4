Chiara gets her big break — and a catch: the studio's biggest project is in Rome, and it means three days a week away from Via dei Tigli. While she packs, Kevin swears he can run the house alone, laundry, ironing and schedules included. In Borgoverde, Franco's wedding plans collide with Lucia's son Gennaro, whose plane lands but whose suitcase, wallet and sense of time do not. Watch for Gennaro arriving two hours late at the station, cheerfully holding up five euros and an empty wallet.
