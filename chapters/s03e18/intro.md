This episode belongs to Chiara: a quiet Sunday morning at Via dei Tigli 14, where she opens her mother Anna's old tin box and sorts through notebooks, photographs and handwriting she hasn't seen in years. Then she finds a sealed envelope, addressed to her, hidden at the bottom — and she says nothing to anyone. Meanwhile Lucia, Anna's old dance-class friend, waits at the empty Centro civico with a postcard in her bag and a promise she has kept for years. Watch for the blue ink and the missing signature.
