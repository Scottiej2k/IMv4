On a foggy Wednesday, a nationwide strike stops every train in Borgoverde. Emma has a school exam in Monza, Chiara has an important meeting in Milan with Marchetti, and nobody can get anywhere — so Kevin appoints himself the family driver, complete with a route plan and a yellow notebook. Between endless traffic jams, a crowded bus stop, and Grandpa Franco's stories at the bar, the day becomes a small adventure. Watch for Emma's goodbye at the school gate: she tells her dad he drives "like an American in Chicago," and he beams and says, "Thank you."
