Leo has a school project: draw his family tree. So he turns reporter, interviewing Dad, Grandpa Franco, Uncle Matteo, and even the neighbor Ornella, filling empty branches with names. Kevin learns a new word for himself, and Nadia’s box stays a mystery. But in Franco’s house, Kevin spots a red scarf that doesn’t belong there — and suddenly the family tree has a secret branch no one is talking about. Watch for that scarf.
