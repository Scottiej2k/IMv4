It’s family-tree week on Via dei Tigli. Leo has a school project called “La mia famiglia,” and he turns into a tiny journalist, asking everyone—Dad Kevin, Grandpa Franco, Uncle Matteo, even Signora Ornella—to fill in the blanks. Kevin learns new Italian words, Chiara finds old photos, and one square on Leo’s chart gets a mysterious “punto interrogativo” beside Nadia’s name, because nobody explains exactly what she is to the family. And on Franco’s chair, a red scarf appears that doesn’t belong to him.
