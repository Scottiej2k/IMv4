Emma's week starts on the crowded Monday train to Monza, where Bianca won't stop asking what the quiet boy with the black instrument case is really like. Meanwhile, Kevin is making a list of everyone on Via dei Tigli — heights, hair, faces and all — in his yellow notebook. The two stories collide at dinner when Leo announces exactly who that boy is. Watch for what Kevin cheerfully writes next to "Signor Colombo," and for the word Leo uses to describe Kevin himself: "grande."
