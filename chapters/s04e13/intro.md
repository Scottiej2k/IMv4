It's a busy week on Via dei Tigli. A glossy new American brunch spot opens in the piazza with five-euro menus and a glowing sign, and Matteo is certain it will steal every last customer from Bar Tigli. Kevin, meanwhile, is trying to keep his Saturday cooking course alive with two students, Emma and Tommaso do some comparing of their own over the hedge, and Franco offers one very Italian piece of advice. Then there's the review: great coffee, and “il bagno è un mistero.” Four stars.
