Kevin Carter and his family move into Via dei Tigli 14. It's their first weekend and first school day: boxes everywhere, a new neighbour watching from her window, two kids facing new classes, and Grandpa Franco, down the street at number 9, saying very little. Kevin has practised three sentences in a notebook, and he's ready to be the friendliest neighbour on the street, with more enthusiasm than Italian. But a confident "Buonasera" to the wrong person at the wrong time lands him in trouble over a parking space marked COLOMBO. What went wrong?
