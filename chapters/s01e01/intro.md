Kevin Carter and his family move into Via dei Tigli 14. It’s their first weekend and first school day: boxes everywhere, a new neighbour watching from her window, two kids facing new classes, and Grandpa Franco, down the street at number 9, saying very little. Kevin knows exactly three Italian phrases, and he tries them on everyone. But when he meets the neighbourhood committee president, a confident “Buonasera” in the afternoon lands him in trouble over a parking space marked COLOMBO. Will three phrases be enough?
