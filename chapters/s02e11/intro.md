Matteo’s Monday delivery goes wrong at Bar Tigli: three hundred croissants, no coffee, and a receipt he signed without looking. Enter Kevin, America’s most enthusiastic fan of lists, who turns the chaotic storeroom into a proper warehouse — while Matteo proposes a “cornetto party.” Meanwhile, Nadia starts a recipe notebook, and two grandmothers battle over eggs, yeast, and cinnamon. Watch for the order that nearly goes wrong again: “Ne voglio cento” — but one hundred of what?
