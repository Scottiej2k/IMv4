Kevin has a secret, and it is sitting in his laptop: a job offer from Chicago that wants him back, with a promotion, double the salary and a contract that needs an answer by February. He tells nobody — not his wife, not his friends at the bar — while the whole street quietly watches him lose his smile. Chiara senses something is wrong, and one small piece of paper on the kitchen table may give her away: a single word, "Chicago", in Kevin's own handwriting.
