It's a hot Sunday afternoon, and Kevin falls asleep in the sun at the pool in Parco dei Tigli — until he turns as red as a tomato. Emma keeps warning him, Leo offers bandaids and cough syrup, and on Monday the whole line at the pharmacy has advice for his sunburn. This is Kevin's week, and he still owes an answer about Sartori. Watch for Grandpa Franco's cure: not a pharmacy cream, but a plate of pasta — with no recipe for anyone.
