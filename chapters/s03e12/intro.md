Nadia’s baby isn’t born yet, but Matteo already has a list: stroller, diapers, car seat. The trouble is the grandmothers — Samira and Lucia each have strong opinions, and one shopping trip turns into a polite little war. Meanwhile, Kevin begs Franco to teach him bocce, and Chiara counts the boxes piling up in her father’s hallway. Watch for Franco’s grudging “un punto” — and for Ornella, who knits every evening without knowing what she’s making.
