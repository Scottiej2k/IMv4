This week it's Kevin's turn to feel lost: the kitchen is full of dust, the builders start at seven, and he keeps saying he's fine while missing his old life in Chicago. Meanwhile Emma reads a message from her friend Mia and realizes something has changed. At the bar, Nadia gently corrects his Italian when he says "Mi manco Chicago" — and across the street, Signora Ornella has one surprising thing to show him: a suitcase that has stayed empty for forty years.
