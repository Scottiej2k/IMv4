It’s a dusty, noisy week on Via dei Tigli. Kevin misses Chicago — his old job, his friends, Sunday pancakes, the lake — and the house is full of builders who keep saying “Nessun problema.” While Emma gets a message from her friend Mia and wonders if she still misses home, Kevin finds an unexpected listener in Signora Ornella. Over coffee and cookies, he discovers an old suitcase in her closet that was packed for Chicago years ago… and never left Italy. What does Ornella know about missing a place you’ve never seen?
