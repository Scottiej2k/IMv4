It's the week of the Festa dei Tigli, and Kevin has been drafted by Roberto Colombo to help organize Borgoverde's biggest street party — six stands, three gazebos, a yellow vest, and a neighbour who thinks everything is a competition. Meanwhile, Emma and Tommaso sneak onto the lottery stand, hoping their two fathers never compare notebooks. And when the microphone comes on, a surprise nobody planned turns into a single tango in front of the whole piazza.
