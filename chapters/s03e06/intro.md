It’s a rainy Saturday in Borgoverde, and eight-year-old Leo has a broken arm, a notebook full of facts, and one big announcement for his dad: he knew about Emma and Tommaso before everyone. At the Bar Tigli, Roberto Colombo arrives with a folder, a theory about trust, and a warning about his hedge. Meanwhile, a tin can from Grandma Anna’s wall holds eight old letters signed only “L.” Watch for Leo’s line “Io lo sapevo prima di tutti” — and the silence it creates.
