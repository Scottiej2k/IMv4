It’s a weekend of secrets on Via dei Tigli. Kevin has known about Emma and Tommaso for months without telling Chiara — and so, it turns out, has eight-year-old Leo, who has a notebook of facts to prove it. Roberto Colombo arrives at the bar with an envelope, a very formal tone, and one firm condition about his hedge. Then Chiara opens Nonna Anna’s old tin can from the wall and finds eight yellowed letters signed only “L.” Who was L? Chiara starts asking questions, but not everyone wants to answer.
