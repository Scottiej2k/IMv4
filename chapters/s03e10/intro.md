Kevin's American parents, Doug and Linda, are in Borgoverde for their anniversary, and Kevin has done his homework. With his yellow notebook open, he books a restaurant in Milan, studies every polite phrase the waiter might use, and proudly orders for the whole table. Back home, Sunday lunch at Franco's brings a new face to the family table. But the real drama comes with the bill: Doug leaves a tip so enormous that the waiter politely hands it back, saying "servizio compreso.
