Kevin Carter’s Monday starts late: his alarm clock says 6:30, but it’s 8:10 in the morning. With the builders already gone, a school trip permission slip missing, and Leo calmly eating breakfast he made himself, Kevin races through Borgoverde apologizing to teachers, neighbors, and committee president Roberto, who sets up his “office” on a folding chair outside. Meanwhile, Leo offers his teacher a very familiar excuse involving Ornella’s cat. Watch for the alarm clock — and what time it actually goes off.
