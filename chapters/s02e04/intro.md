It's Monday morning in Borgoverde, and Kevin Carter wakes up late — very late. His alarm is set for 6:30 p.m., the builders have vanished, and his son Leo has already made breakfast. Between a forgotten school permission slip and a neighborhood committee meeting he missed, Kevin's day becomes a comedy of small disasters. Watch for the moment a teacher reveals that Leo's excuse about Ornella's cat eating his homework has been used by three different kids — same cat, same story.
