# Ordinal numbers, years and centuries

## What it is

Three little systems that all live in the same corner of Italian: how you say *first, second, third*; how you say a year like *1975*; and how you talk about a century (*il Novecento*) or a floor of a building (*al secondo piano*). In this episode Kevin, Leo and Ornella use all three constantly, because the box in the wall is dated 1975 and the street was built in 1962.

## How it works

### Ordinal numbers

Ordinals answer the question *which one in the order?* They behave like normal adjectives and agree with the noun: **la prima volta**, **i primi anni**, **il secondo piano**, **le terze classi**.

The first ten are irregular and must be memorized:

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | **primo** | 6th | **sesto** |
| 2nd | **secondo** | 7th | **settimo** |
| 3rd | **terzo** | 8th | **ottavo** |
| 4th | **quarto** | 9th | **nono** |
| 5th | **quinto** | 10th | **decimo** |

From 11 up, you take the number, drop the final vowel, and add **-esimo**: *undici → undicesimo*, *venti → ventesimo*, *ventuno → ventunesimo*, *cento → centesimo*. These do not change for gender, and they are usually written as one word.

Note: in everyday Italian people often use the cardinal number where English uses an ordinal: *il capitolo due* ("chapter two").

### Years

Years are said as one long number, and written without commas or dots:

- **1975** → **millenovecentosettantacinque**
- **1962** → **millenovecentosessantadue**
- **2000** → **duemila**

When you say *in* a year, use **nel** (or **del**, **dal**, **fino al**, depending on the sentence): **nel 1975**, **del 1968**, **dal 1962 al 1970**.

Decades: **gli anni Settanta**, **gli anni Sessanta** (*the seventies, the sixties*). You can also write **gli anni '70**, but you still say *gli anni Settanta*.

### Centuries

Italian has two ways to name a century, and both are normal:

- **il Novecento** (literally "the nine hundreds") = the 1900s
- **il ventesimo secolo** = the twentieth century

So: **l'Ottocento** = the 1800s / the nineteenth century; **il Settecento** = the 1700s; **il Duemila** or **il ventunesimo secolo** = the 2000s / the twenty-first century. The century word is capitalized, and it takes the article: *nel Novecento*, *dell'Ottocento*.

### Floors

**il piano terra** is the ground floor. Above it: **il primo piano**, **il secondo piano**, **il terzo piano**. To say where someone lives: **abito al secondo piano**.

## From the story

[[Millenovecentosettantacinque]] — Sergio reads the date on the sheet: the year is said as one single word.
[[È del millenovecentosessantotto.]] — Ornella dates Gino's record; **del** + year means "from (the year)".
[[Nel millenovecentosessantadue hanno costruito le prime case di Via dei Tigli.]] — **nel** + year for "in", and the ordinal **prime** agreeing with *case*.
[[Il secondo è arrivato nel millenovecentosessantatré. È il numero nove.]] — an ordinal used alone, as a noun: *il secondo* = "the second one".
[[Un secolo è cento anni.]] — the definition, plain and simple: a century is a hundred years.
[[Nel Novecento, Leo. Tutto il Novecento va dal millenovecento al duemila.]] — the century name, plus the range *dal … al …*.
[[La strada è del millenovecentosessantadue. Ma il millenovecentosessantadue è in quale secolo?]] — the same year used twice, first with **del**, then alone as the subject of the question.
[[Guarda qui. Questa è Via dei Tigli nel millenovecentosettantacinque.]] — **nel** + year again, in a photo caption.
[[Quasi mezzo secolo.]] — *secolo* in a fraction phrase: "almost half a century".

## Common mistakes

- **Reading years digit by digit.** English says "nineteen seventy-five"; Italian says **millenovecentosettantacinque**. *Diciannove settantacinque* is not Italian.
- **Adding "the" where Italian doesn't.** *Nel 1975* is correct; *al 1975* is not.
- **Forgetting agreement on ordinals.** *La prima volta*, *il secondo piano*, *le prime case* — the ending follows the noun.
- **Using the cardinal for cents and pages, but the ordinal for centuries.** *Il Novecento*, *il ventesimo secolo* — never *il venti secolo*.
- **Forgetting that Italian floors start above the ground floor.** A *secondo piano* in Italy is often the third level of the building.
- **Capitalizing years but not centuries.** Years are lowercase (**millenovecentosessantotto**); century names take a capital (**il Novecento**).

## Practice

1. Write in words: *1962*.
2. Write in words: *2000*.
3. Complete: *Il nostro appartamento è ____ terzo piano.*
4. Complete with **nel** or **del**: *Questa foto è ____ 1975.*
5. Say the century: *il 1900* → *____*
6. Make the ordinal agree: *la (primo) volta che vedo questa scrittura*.
7. Translate: *The street was built in 1962, in the twentieth century.*
8. Rewrite with an ordinal number: *il piano dopo il primo piano* → *____*

**Answer key**

1. millenovecentosessantadue
2. duemila
3. al (*Il nostro appartamento è al terzo piano.*)
4. del (*Questa foto è del 1975.*)
5. il Novecento (or: il ventesimo secolo)
6. la prima volta
7. La strada è stata costruita nel 1962, nel Novecento.
8. il secondo piano
