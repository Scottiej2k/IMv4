The Carter kitchen renovation ends with a surprise: Leo and Kevin find a metal box sealed inside the old wall. Inside are photos and handwritten letters from 1975. Ornella recognizes the handwriting at once — it belongs to Anna Ferri, Chiara's mother, gone for three years now. Later, Franco asks Kevin to keep the box, and the truth, from Chiara a little longer. Watch for the small yellow page Ornella picks up at the kitchen table: one look and she has to sit down.
