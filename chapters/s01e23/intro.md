It's February in Borgoverde, and the street wakes up under a rare snowfall — real snow, enough to close the schools and bury the cars. Kevin is thrilled, Leo runs outside in his pajamas, and Emma keeps checking the trains. Across the street, Franco has a proverb for every flake, while Kevin shovels beside the silent Roberto Colombo. Meanwhile, Emma and Leo are writing “l'elenco di papà,” a strict list of what Dad always, often, sometimes, or never does. Watch what happens at line number three.
