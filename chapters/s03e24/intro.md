It's proposal week on Via dei Tigli. Franco wants to ask Lucia to marry him, with a gold ring, a letter and a plan like a fairy tale — but he admits to Kevin that his hands shake and his voice trembles, and he needs two days and total secrecy. Meanwhile, Emma and Tommaso must tell the story of how they met at the school assembly, and they can't agree on one single detail. Watch for the small tin box Franco hides behind the pear tree, and for Kevin's jacket pocket, which suddenly feels very heavy.
