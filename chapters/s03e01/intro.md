A new school year begins on Via dei Tigli, and this episode belongs to Chiara. Kevin proudly hands out snacks, Leo waits for recess, and Chiara finally opens the old tin — a “latta” — that Kevin found hidden in her father's wall two years ago and never mentioned. Inside are her mother's photographs, a cloth doll, and questions Franco would rather leave in the attic. Meanwhile, boxes start piling up at Franco's door. Watch for four boxes, three flowerpots, and one dinner nobody planned.
