It's the first day of school, and Kevin is packing snacks nobody asked for. But this episode belongs to Chiara: in Franco's kitchen an old, dusty tin appears — the one found hidden inside a wall back in August. Inside are her mother's doll, an album of photographs, and a secret Kevin never mentioned. Meanwhile Lucia keeps arriving with boxes, and eight-year-old Leo wants to hear every game the grown-ups played "quando ero piccola." Watch for the tin, and for who knew about it first.
