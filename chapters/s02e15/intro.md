It's Saturday on Via dei Tigli, and Kevin finally feels ready: he's baked his late mother-in-law's lemon cake for Signora Ornella Galli's birthday, with a small package besides. After eight months of formal "Lei" and "signora Galli," he's invited into her kitchen — where the candles, the cream, and the words themselves get complicated. Watch for the moment Ornella smiles and says "Diamoci del tu," and for Kevin's mouth, which keeps going back to "La ringrazio" no matter how hard he tries.
