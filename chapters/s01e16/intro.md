It's Saturday morning on Via dei Tigli, and Roberto Colombo arrives at Kevin's gate with a thirty-page rulebook for the street — and a pen. Kevin signs without reading a word, then sets out to become the perfect neighbor: right bin, right day, right hedge. Meanwhile, Chiara isn't speaking to her father, Franco, and Sunday lunch has an empty chair. Watch for Wednesday, when Kevin finds every trash bin on the street parked outside his own house.
