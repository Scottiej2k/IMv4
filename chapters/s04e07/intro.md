Franco and Lucia are getting married in June, and Chiara has three polished reception venues ready to show them: a grand villa in Monza, a restaurant in the piazza, and the hall at the Centro civico. Franco has just two questions for every one of them — who serves at the table, and who blows out the candles? He answers them with a tailor's measuring tape, a garden, and a very stubborn opinion. Watch for the thirteen-pound guest who shows up at the orchard without an invitation.
