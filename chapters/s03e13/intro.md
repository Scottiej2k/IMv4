It's Christmas Eve, and a snowstorm has buried Borgoverde. At Bar Tigli, Matteo is about to close—until Nadia arrives with regular contractions. With trains on strike and the roads a sheet of ice, Kevin drives Nadia toward the hospital while Samira and Lucia argue about cribs, strollers, and diapers. Matteo, stuck with a customer who sips coffee forever, races to catch up. Watch for a blue bow that flies into the wind, and the three-word message that makes everyone freeze: "Sta per nascere!"
