It's Christmas Eve, and in Borgoverde nothing goes as planned: Nadia is having contractions at Bar Tigli, and the road to the hospital is a mess of snow, ice, and traffic. Kevin drives, Franco gives directions, and Samira and Lucia argue in the back seat while Nadia counts the minutes. Meanwhile, Matteo is still stuck at the bar with a customer who drinks his coffee impossibly slowly. He finally grabs a box of diapers and a little blue bow and runs out into the storm.
