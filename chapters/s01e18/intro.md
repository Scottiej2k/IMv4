Kevin has a five-point plan for the perfect morning: wake up, get up, brush his teeth, shower, and make breakfast for everyone. The problem? One bathroom, four people, and a train to Monza at 7:30. Across the street, Leo tries Grandpa Franco's own morning recipe instead, tomato and bocce included. And every night, Pavarotti the cat sleeps on Kevin's side of the bed. Watch for Leo's question about whether "mezzo punto" really exists — and for what he does in the middle of a sentence that evening.
