Sunday lunch at Franco's is a test for Kevin, who has been practising Italian all morning — and who answers every single question with the same three words. Meanwhile Leo meets Signora Ornella on Via dei Tigli, helps her carry her shopping home, and discovers that her big, quiet house has no grandchildren in it. What Leo proposes to fix this, over biscuits and a glass of water, is not something anyone in the family expects: a "contratto" involving shopping bags, biscuits and a new name for Ornella.
