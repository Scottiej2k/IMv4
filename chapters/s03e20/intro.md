It's a big week on Via dei Tigli: Chiara's boss and his wife are coming to dinner, and Chiara wants everything perfect. Kevin, armed with his yellow notebook of rules, cooks fish and plans an American apple dessert. Emma, meanwhile, tells Tommaso she loves his rock band and that she's a great skater — two cheerful lies that will catch up with her. Then the doorbell rings a second time, and Lucia is standing there with a cake and the whole Ferri family behind her. So much for a quiet dinner.
