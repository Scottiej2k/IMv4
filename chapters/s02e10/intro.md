This week it's Kevin's turn to face Italian bureaucracy. The new window in the family's house needs a permit, and Roberto Colombo, president of the neighborhood committee, arrives with a paper, a rule and a lecture. So Kevin heads to the town hall in Borgoverde with his yellow notebook, while Chiara works late in Milan on her own deadline. Between long lines, closed offices and the construction boss Sergio's answer — "Ci vogliono due settimane" — Kevin learns what a window can really cost. Watch for the moment someone reads his notebook over his shoulder and discovers it's upside down.
