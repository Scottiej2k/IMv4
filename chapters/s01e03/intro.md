Moving day at Via dei Tigli 14. Chiara's family fills the new house with boxes labelled in English, and eight-year-old Leo decides that every object needs an Italian name — starting with a post-it stuck on his own forehead. Ornella's grey cat Pavarotti turns up as an "ospite" and settles on the garden table, the sofa and the boxes. But when everyone wants the first coffee in the new home, the box marked CAFFÈ turns out to be completely empty.
