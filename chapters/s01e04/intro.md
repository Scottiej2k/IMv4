Kevin and Leo set out on a Saturday to map their new neighborhood. With a yellow notebook, they collect squares, churches, fountains and bars, ask directions, and slowly learn that Borgoverde has more than one bar and plenty of rules. Kevin just wants to feel like a neighbor. At the bakery, his simple request for bread earns a very Italian correction: what could be wrong with asking for "un chilo di pane"? Meanwhile, Chiara commutes to Milan and faces delays of her own.
