Kevin is learning bocce, and Franco, his father-in-law, is a very reluctant teacher. On the court in Parco dei Tigli, Kevin's arm is too fast, his breathing too loud, and his ball keeps disappearing into the woods. Franco says only one word: "piano." Meanwhile, the doctor tells Nadia to slow down, and Matteo does the exact opposite. Keep an eye on Franco's old notebook, where Kevin's name goes on a list — but which list?
