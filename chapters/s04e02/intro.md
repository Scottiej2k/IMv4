This week on Via dei Tigli, everyone is thinking about the future. Kevin is invited to Leo’s class for Profession Day, where a bridge engineer and a lawyer make him wonder what his coffee-shop work is really worth. Emma visits a school orientation fair in Monza and faces the same question, while Grandpa Franco cheerfully predicts everyone’s destiny. Keep an eye out for Leo’s first cappuccino at Bar Tigli: it looks less like coffee and more like soup. Will anyone dare to drink it?
