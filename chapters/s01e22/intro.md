It's a big Sunday in Borgoverde: Bar Tigli's first brunch, and Kevin is in charge of the coffee machine — all twenty buttons of it. Meanwhile, Chiara goes to the community center to meet Lucia, her father's dance teacher, and learns something she never knew about her own mother: the family tiramisù has a secret. Sunday lunch with Franco is coming, and Chiara plans to search her mother's kitchen for a certain recipe list.
