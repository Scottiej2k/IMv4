It's almost Franco's 72nd birthday, and Kevin has a plan: twenty-two guests, ten red balloons, and a surprise nobody on Via dei Tigli can manage to keep. With a yellow notebook and a phone that will not stop ringing, the family creeps across the street before dawn, while Chiara makes tiramisù with Lucia and Franco watches everything from his garden, suspicious of every "innocent" errand on the street. Watch for the moment the party nearly ends in one word shouted by Leo — and for the place Kevin leaves his shoe.
