It's the weekend before Franco's seventy-second birthday, and Kevin is planning a surprise party — at six in the morning, whispering into his phone so he won't wake the house. But Franco misses nothing: he stands in his garden watching the street, counting every phone call and every buzz of his intercom. Meanwhile Chiara and Lucia make Anna's tiramisù, twenty-two guests wait, and Leo can't keep a secret for two seconds. Watch for a shoe in the refrigerator — and an intercom that nobody should touch.
