It's market day in Borgoverde, and Kevin Carter is determined to master the Tuesday market with his yellow notebook. Franco comes along, teaching him weights, prices, and the unwritten rule that a customer looks at the fruit but never touches it. Meanwhile, Matteo dreams up an all-American brunch at Bar Tigli, and Leo helps shop for thirty customers. Watch for the moment Matteo rolls a supermarket cart right into the bar — and for Franco's surprising little gift of apples the next morning.
