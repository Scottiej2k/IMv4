It's Valentine's Day in Borgoverde, and Franco has a secret he's terrible at hiding. Kevin, who can't keep a secret to save his life, knows the plan; Chiara senses something is up; and Matteo is convinced his heart-shaped croissants will save the bar. At the community center dance, with the whole neighborhood watching, Franco takes the microphone, unfolds a letter, and tries to kneel. But when the big question comes, the answer arrives from the wrong person in the room. Find out who can't help shouting.
