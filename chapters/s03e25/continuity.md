### s03e25 · San Valentino
- **Happened:** At the Centro civico's Valentine's party, Franco read a letter to Lucia and proposed on one knee with an old ring; the whole room watched. Chiara shouted "Sì!" before Lucia answered, then Lucia said yes. Next morning Franco and Lucia visited the Carters, and later had a coffee at Bar Tigli.
- **New facts:** Lucia's full name is Lucia Bernardi; Franco's ring is from 1978 ("due stipendi") and was Anna's. Franco is 72 and has a bad right knee. Leo now calls Lucia *nonna Lucia*; Chiara called her *mamma*. Kevin writes new words in a yellow notebook. Matteo's Valentine's heart-shaped cornetti were left unsold.
- **Changed:** Franco and Lucia are engaged, publicly. Kevin had known about the proposal for a week and helped with the letter; Nadia now knows he kept it quiet. Chiara and Kevin plan to dance together in the kitchen every Saturday.
- **Planted:** The wedding date is undecided — Franco says the decision is his.
