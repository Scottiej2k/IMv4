It's Sunday lunch at Nonno Franco's, and one question about summer holidays turns the whole family into detectives. Chiara and Matteo remember the very same childhood vacations completely differently: the car, the route, even the boarding house. Kevin takes notes in his yellow notebook; Leo signs his grandfather up for Grandparents' Day at school without asking. Then, in Franco's hallway, someone spots a dusty shoebox with a handwritten receipt on top — and a seashell that still has sand inside.
