Sunday lunch at Franco's brings the whole family to the table, but the meal turns into a Ferri debate: Chiara and Matteo can't agree on anything about their childhood summers—the car, the route, the songs, even the name of their seaside boarding house. Kevin listens, takes notes, and learns a new word. Meanwhile, Leo invites his grandfather to school for Grandparents' Day, where Franco's memories of 1961 surprise everyone. Watch for a shoebox of photos, a seashell with sand still inside, and a handwritten receipt that might settle everything—or start a new argument.
