Kevin’s big idea is a logo for his morning greeting, and he is sure a shop in the piazza can make him one — until Matteo and Nadia start asking how much it costs. Meanwhile, Franco insists that his wedding suit must come from the tailor who measured him in 1975, and Lucia quietly refuses to let anyone see her cake. Watch for the secret drawing of a coffee cup with a linden leaf inside it, and for exactly what Lucia finally shows everyone on a table at home.
