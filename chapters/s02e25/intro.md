It’s Ferragosto, and on Via dei Tigli the Carter family finally has a working kitchen. Kevin wants to celebrate, Franco wants to grill, and everyone has a different version of the eight-month renovation: perfect, flooded, or crooked. As the family gathers in Franco’s garden for sausages, watermelon, and a toast to those who aren’t there, Emma slips away with a midnight promise to Tommaso under the moon. But a certain neighbor in his pajamas is watching from an upstairs window—and he has something to say.
