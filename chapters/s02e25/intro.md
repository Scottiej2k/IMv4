It's August 14, almost a year after the Carters arrived on Via dei Tigli, and the hammering has finally stopped. Kevin scrubs the last dust from his brand-new kitchen, then joins the whole street for Ferragosto in Franco's garden, where the sausages get burned once again. Everyone tells the story of the house their own way — Kevin's version is very short. And late that night, by the hedge, Emma makes a promise to Tommaso under the full moon. Then a window opens upstairs.
