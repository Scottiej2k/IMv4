It’s the quiet week after Christmas in Borgoverde, and baby Anna Yasmin is only days old. Lucia fills Franco’s house with “paroline”—little words for tiny hands and feet—while Leo records every one in his notebook, and Kevin takes over the early mornings at Bar Tigli so an exhausted Matteo can sleep. Franco claims he’s too afraid to hold the newborn. But watch for the moment a certain tough grandfather is caught using a very small word himself.
