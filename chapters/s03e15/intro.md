It's the quiet week between Christmas and New Year on Via dei Tigli, and the whole Ferri-Carter clan is gathered around the newest arrival: baby Anna Yasmin. Franco claims he's too scared to hold her, Leo keeps a secret notebook counting every one of Lucia's little words, and Kevin takes over the early mornings at Bar Tigli, where a rushed customer asks for a big cappuccino and gets something with a brand-new name: "cappuccinone." Watch for it.
