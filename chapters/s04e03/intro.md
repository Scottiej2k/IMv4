Rain falls on Borgoverde, and two mysteries keep the neighborhood busy. At Bar Tigli, Kevin can't stop watching a silent stranger who drinks coffee, reads a magazine and writes in a little notebook — is he a food critic, a tax inspector, a rival? Meanwhile, Lucia hasn't slept: her son Gennaro was due to land last night, but he never called. Franco stays calm, the neighbors gather, and everyone has a theory. Listen for the moment a phone turns up at the bottom of an airport toilet — and the six messages waiting for it.
