Kevin's parents, Doug and Linda, arrive from Ohio for their first visit to Borgoverde, and Kevin is determined that everything go perfectly. At the train station he gives orders like a stationmaster, while Chiara stays calm and Roberto gets drafted to carry luggage. At home, Franco rehearses an English speech to welcome Kevin's American parents, and the family gathers for Sunday lunch. Watch for the moment Franco proudly stands and says 'Good morning. Welcome to my house'—and for Kevin's favorite new phrase, 'Sbrigatevi!' The jet lag, the old tablecloth, and one nervous grandfather make it a memorable welcome.
