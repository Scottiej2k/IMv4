### s01e08 · Halloween in Via dei Tigli
- **Happened:** Kevin fills the garden at number 14 with a two-metre plastic pumpkin, twenty small pumpkins, candles and candy bags; Roberto delivers letters invoking "la regola numero dodici" of the Comitato against it. Franco takes Kevin to Borgoverde's cemetery on Ognissanti (Sunday, 1 November, the morning after Halloween night) to Anna Ferri's grave, where Kevin adds his flowers to the vase. Roberto's objection is left unresolved.
- **New facts:** Marina is Roberto's wife and slips five candies to Leo and Emma through the window; Tommaso carries a black guitar case; Kevin is 41; Ornella brings flowers to the grave of her late husband Gino; Franco keeps the same metal vase on Anna's grave and has done for ten years, then eats lunch alone.
- **Changed:** Kevin sees where Franco's late wife Anna lies and speaks to her («Signora Anna, piacere di conoscerla»); Franco, still calling him *l'americano*, tells Kevin to keep the pumpkin because Anna liked parties and Americans. Roberto half-suspects the candies.
- **Planted:** Tommaso–Emma runway encounter; Leo asks to do Halloween again next year.
