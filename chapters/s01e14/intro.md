It's Christmas Eve on Via dei Tigli, and Kevin is nervous. He has set the table for six, but nobody in the family can say who the sixth guest is — Kevin just repeats, "Un amico del corso d'italiano." At Franco's, the fish is in the oven, the pandoro is out, and a woman in a red coat arrives at the door. Meanwhile Emma slips away to midnight mass. Watch for a quiet thank-you from Franco that leaves Kevin speechless in the hallway.
