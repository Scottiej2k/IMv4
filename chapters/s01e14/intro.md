It’s Christmas Eve in Borgoverde, and Kevin is setting the table for seven—but only six people are coming. Who is the mysterious seventh guest? Kevin claims it’s “un amico del corso d’italiano,” a friend from his Italian class, but nobody believes him. At Franco’s house, the fish is in the oven and the pandoro is on the table. Emma slips away to midnight mass, where Tommaso is waiting. Watch for the doorbell: a woman in a red coat arrives with a huge cake, and Franco starts nervously moving the forks.
