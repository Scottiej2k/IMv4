It's a week of secrets on Via dei Tigli. Every morning Emma and Tommaso trade one shy «ciao» at the train station while Bianca watches and asks uncomfortable questions. Franco has a secret too: he and Lucia are a couple, but only behind closed doors. So he makes a promise — to walk down the street holding her hand. What he forgets is that Ornella watches the street from her window, and she has something loud to say.
