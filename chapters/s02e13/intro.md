Everyone on Via dei Tigli is keeping a secret. Emma and Tommaso act like strangers at the train station every morning, though they write to each other all night, and their friend Bianca is starting to notice. Meanwhile, Franco has been hiding his relationship with Lucia for months — until he finally promises to walk down the street holding her hand. When their neighbor Ornella leans out of her window and shouts “Che bella coppia!”, the whole street suddenly knows. Can Franco survive being seen in public?
