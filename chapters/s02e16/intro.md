Leo has a school assignment: interview the whole family about Italy and America, and decide which one wins. Easy? Not in the Carter house, where Kevin answers every question with a pen and a notepad, Emma votes for Italian pizza and longer words, and Grandpa Franco has strong opinions about bread, cheese, and coffee. When a customer at Bar Tigli orders an American-style coffee, the debate gets even louder. Watch for Kevin's notebook and for Franco's quiet line: "Grande non è meglio.
