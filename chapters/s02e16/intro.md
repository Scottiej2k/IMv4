Leo has a school presentation called “Italy and America,” so he interviews his whole family about food, coffee, houses, and holidays. In the kitchen, at Franco’s vegetable garden, and at Bar Tigli, every question turns into a lively debate. Kevin insists America wins every round, Chiara refuses to pick a winner, and Franco has a quiet answer that surprises everyone. Watch for the moment Kevin proudly says “più meglio” and Emma immediately corrects him.
