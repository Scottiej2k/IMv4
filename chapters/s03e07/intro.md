In this episode, Chiara finds something unexpected in her father's kitchen drawer: her mother's old notebook, full of lasagne and sauce and one mysterious instruction — "quanto basta." Chiara decides to make Sunday lunch all by herself, no help from anyone, while across the street Kevin and Leo try pancakes with a cast on Leo's arm and send flour flying everywhere. Watch for the moment Kevin writes "quanto basta" in his own little notebook, and for Franco's first bite of the lasagne.
