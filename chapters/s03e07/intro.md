It's Saturday morning on Via dei Tigli, and Chiara finds something unexpected in her father's kitchen drawer: her mother's old recipe notebook, stained with tomato sauce. The recipes have no measurements — just "quanto basta." Chiara decides to make the lasagne herself, alone. But her father, Lucia, and Kevin have other ideas, and by Sunday the kitchen fills with more than just the smell of sauce. Watch for Kevin's little notebook, where one mysterious phrase gets a giant question mark.
