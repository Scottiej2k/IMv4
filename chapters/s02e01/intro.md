The Carters' first morning in the new house on Via dei Tigli 14 — and Kevin already has a yellow notebook, builders in yellow helmets, and a plan. Chiara has strong opinions about the kitchen; eight-year-old Leo wants to know if a construction site is like a farm. Across the street, Grandpa Franco knows this old house better than anyone, and he turns up at seven in the morning with a hammer, a measuring tape, and fifty years of tram stories. Watch for Leo's proud new collection: three glass jars of construction dust.
