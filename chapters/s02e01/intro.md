It's Monday morning on Via dei Tigli, and Kevin Carter can't stop smiling: the house at number 14 is finally his. He's called the builders, signed the papers, and dreams of a gleaming new kitchen. One small problem — his father-in-law Franco, who knows every wall on this street, was never asked. Meanwhile, at Bar Tigli, Matteo's new coffee and sixteen designer glasses are quietly wrecking his accounts. Watch for Franco turning up at seven in the morning with a hammer and a spiral notebook from 1978.
