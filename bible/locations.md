# Locations

Location **ids** go in `scenes[].location` in `chapter.json`.

## Borgoverde (fictional)

A town of about 20,000 people, 20 km north of Milan, between Monza and the Brianza hills. It has
tree-lined residential streets, a small historic centre with a church and a piazza, a station on the
suburban line (*linea S*) with trains to Milano Porta Garibaldi every 30 minutes (about 25 minutes'
ride), and a park with bocce courts.
