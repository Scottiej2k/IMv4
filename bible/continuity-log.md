# Continuity log

Read this before writing a chapter. After a chapter is final, add one entry. Keep entries short and
factual: this file is what keeps 200 chapters consistent.

## Entry template

```
### s01e01 · <Italian title>
- **Happened:** 2–4 bullets of plot.
- **New facts:** names, places, ages, possessions, relationships, anything later chapters must respect.
- **Changed:** relationship shifts, secrets now known (and by whom), open threads.
- **Planted:** set-ups that need a payoff later (with the planned episode if known).
```

## State at series start (before S1E1)

- The Carters arrive from Chicago the weekend before school starts, in September of year 1.
- Via dei Tigli 14 is rented at first. The family buys it in S2 (renovation arc).
- Franco doesn't know Ben well: they have met on four summer visits, with no shared language.
- Only Franco and Lucia know about Franco and Lucia.
- Bar Tigli is losing money. Matteo owes Franco part of the purchase price.
- Nadia is not yet pregnant.
- Emma and Tommaso have never met.
- Ornella's cat is called **Pavarotti**.

## Episodes

### s01e01 · Il primo giorno
- **Happened:** The Carters move boxes into Via dei Tigli 14 on a Saturday; Ben meets Ornella, Franco and Roberto while a white van blocks Roberto's parking space. Roberto makes Chiara move the van; Ornella leaves a cake and a «Benvenuti!» note. Monday: Leo starts terza B at scuola primaria Gianni Rodari with maestra Paola, befriends Pietro; Emma takes the train to Monza with Bianca of seconda B; Franco leaves a bag of tomatoes «Per Leo» at number 14.
- **New facts:** Ornella Galli lives at 16 with grey cat Pavarotti; Roberto Colombo lives at 11 and is comitato president; his parking space is marked COLOMBO on the wall; Roberto's number 11 has a small parcheggio; Franco grows tomatoes; Leo is in terza B, maestra Paola; Emma in seconda B (Monza), Bianca a new classmate; Chiara works in Milan.
- **Changed:** Franco stays cold with Ben but warms to Leo.
- **Planted:** Emma's silence and difficulty settling (thread to continue); Franco/Lucia still secret.

### s01e02 · Un caffè, per favore
- **Happened:** Ben learns Italian coffee etiquette from Matteo (cappuccino only in the morning, brioche = cornetto in the north, you pay *after*), and meets Franco's four-course Sunday lunch; Franco calls him "l'americano," which Ben mishears as *caffè americano* and takes as a compliment.
- **New facts:** Ben keeps a small yellow notebook, numbering one new word per day (nineteen words by Sunday evening); he writes *l'americano* and "mangia bene" in it. Sunday lunch at Franco's is at 12:30, pasta–carne–frutta–dolce; Franco's late wife's dessert was a lemon cake. Bar Tigli: Matteo behind the counter, a caffè and cornetto cost €1,20 on Monday (€2,50 on Saturday). Ben resolves to drink his coffee standing at the bar each morning.
- **Changed:** Franco's attitude to Ben thaws slightly ("L'americano mangia bene"); Matteo calls Ben a friend/Unofficial Italian.
- **Planted:** Ben's standing-at-the-bar routine and growing notebook (Matteo: "Domani ancora?").

### s01e03 · La casa nuova
- **Happened:** The Carters unpack boxes in their rented house at Via dei Tigli 14; Ben labels everything in English, Leo writes Italian post-its (MURO, EMMA, CAFFÈ, PAVAROTTI, CASA). Ornella leaves her cat Pavarotti in their garden for the afternoon; Chiara and Leo find the missing coffee machine in a garden box. Franco brings tomatoes for Leo on Saturday evening and meets the cat.
- **New facts:** The house is rented; kitchen is small ("cucina piccola"), three bedrooms, one bathroom, a living room, garden with a small table, old wall and old wardrobe. Their coffee machine is small; Ben keeps a yellow notebook of mistakes (this is error number twenty: *camera* → "macchina fotografica"). Ornella's cat is called Pavarotti (grey, sings, sleeps everywhere). Franco knows the flat is rented, which is news to him.
- **Changed:** none; Ben/Ornella remain on Lei.
- **Planted:** Chiara's wish to buy a new house someday; an unopened box under the wardrobe; Franco's disapproval of the rented house.

### s01e05 · Quanti anni hai?
- **Happened:** Sunday lunch at Franco's; Ben answers family questions with "Ho fame" until prompted; Franco reveals the photo is of Anna, dead three years. Leo helps Ornella home, learns she is an only child with no children or grandchildren, and proposes a "contract": he carries her bags, she gives biscuits, he calls her "nonna." Monday: at the bar Franco asks Ben "Milan o Inter?"
- **New facts:** Ornella is 78, an only child, childless, widow of Gino (ran the bar 40 years); she has a photo of herself young with her parents; cat Pavarotti. Leo is 8, Ben 41, Matteo 36, Franco 71. Ben's relatives in Ohio: mother, father, two brothers, one sister, two uncles, one aunt, four cousins. Ornella gives Leo a photo of her and Gino.
- **Changed:** Ornella and Leo now call each other "nonna"/"nipote" (contract); Chiara, Ben and Emma know of it. Chiara, Matteo and Ben notice Franco's vague Sunday "corso" at the Centro civico.
- **Planted:** Franco's Sunday course (Lucia, still secret); Ornella's loneliness as Leo's chosen family; Emma's promised photo of Leo and Ornella; Franco's Milan-Inter question left unanswered by Ben.

### s05e03 · Vogliono che torni
- **Happened:** Dave Miller, Ben's old Chicago boss, emails a job offer: promotion to marketing director, double salary, three-year contract, relocation in March, reply by Friday. Ben tells Ornella, who advises telling Chiara; he doesn't, and sends Dave only a request for a week. Chiara has been asked to work in Rome three days a week for two months, starting Monday.
- **New facts:** Dave Miller (Ben's former boss, two daughters, a dog) and Marchetti (Chiara's colleague) are named. Franco is 74 and drove the same tram line 35 years; he refused an office job. Ornella's husband Gino refused a Turin transfer before buying the bar (1968).
- **Changed:** Ornella knows about the Chicago offer; Matteo, Nadia and Emma only suspect Ben is hiding something. Chiara found Ben's note reading "Chicago" and knows he lies about work.
- **Planted:** Ben must answer Dave by Friday; Chiara will demand the truth again; Ornella's open door for Ben.
